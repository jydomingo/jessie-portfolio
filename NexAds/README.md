# NexAds

NexAds is a standalone, multi-tenant AI marketing product within the Nodeya ecosystem.

[Read the full case study](CASE_STUDY.md)

## Portfolio scope

- Campaign management
- AI-assisted content generation
- Analytics and reporting
- Marketing automation
- CRM and brand management
- Team collaboration
- Enterprise administration

## Technology profile

ASP.NET Core, React, TypeScript, REST APIs, multi-tenancy, AI provider abstractions, and automation foundations.
