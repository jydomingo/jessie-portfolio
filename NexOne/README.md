# NexOne

NexOne is Nodeya’s flagship multi-tenant enterprise resource planning platform.

[Read the full case study](CASE_STUDY.md)

## Portfolio scope

- Accounting and finance
- CRM, sales, and purchasing
- Inventory and manufacturing
- Human resources and payroll
- Projects and asset management
- Document management and reporting
- Workflow automation
- Tenant management and integrations

## Technology profile

ASP.NET Core, React, TypeScript, REST APIs, PostgreSQL/SQL Server, OIDC/JWT, Docker, Clean Architecture, DDD, and CQRS.
