# Case Study: Eljin Employee Portal

## Executive summary

The Eljin Employee Portal is a secured internal application intended to centralize employee communication, digital forms, approvals, learning resources, support requests, events, and frequently requested information.

## The challenge

Internal information and requests can become fragmented across messages, paper forms, spreadsheets, and separate communication channels. Employees need one authenticated location for announcements, forms, approvals, learning content, support, and organizational information, while administrators need controlled content and workflow management.

## My role

IT Supervisor and Software Developer responsible for:

- Business requirements and feature planning
- Application and access-control direction
- Digital workflow and approval design
- Dashboard and content-management scope
- Coordination with broader IT operations and standards
- Security, supportability, and continuity considerations

## Solution approach

The portal combines authentication, announcements, digital forms and approvals, an employee dashboard, content management, organization and event information, learning resources, ticketing, notifications, and FAQ navigation.

Access control protects internal functions, while structured content and workflows reduce reliance on scattered communication and manual routing. The application is designed as part of a broader effort to improve internal technology operations and business continuity.

## Portfolio outcome

The project demonstrates end-to-end ownership from business requirements and workflow analysis through application design and operational support. It also shows how software development and IT leadership can work together to improve employee-facing services.

## Status and confidentiality

This case study intentionally remains at a public portfolio level. Employer source code, employee information, internal screenshots, credentials, security details, and operational configuration are not included.
